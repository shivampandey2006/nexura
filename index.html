Butter Paneer Masala

A rich, creamy and delicious Indian curry made with paneer, tomatoes, butter and aromatic spices.

Preparation: 15 minutes

Cooking: 25 minutes

Servings: 3-4 people

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer
  • 3 medium tomatoes
  • 1 medium onion
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon cream
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt as needed

Preparation Steps

  1. Cut the paneer into small cubes.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and lightly fry the paneer.
  3. Add chopped onion, tomatoes and ginger-garlic paste.
  4. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  5. Add turmeric, chilli powder, salt and garam masala.
  6. Blend the cooked mixture into a smooth gravy.
  7. Pour the gravy back into the pan and add the paneer.
  8. Cook for 5 minutes and add fresh cream.
  9. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.

Nutrition Information

Nutrient Amount
Calories 320 kcal
Protein 15 g
Carbohydrates 12 g
Fat 22 g
